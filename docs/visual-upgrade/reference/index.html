142 original screenshots · 4 transition recordings · local baseline 958584d4

Original game references, not redesign concepts. QA warps and isolated saves enable complete coverage. Gameplay pairs share the same simulation state. Screen-left means decreasing X; screen-right means increasing X. Videos are frame-stepped and include their boot lead-in; they do not establish real-time performance.
